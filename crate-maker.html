Turn a folder of sounds into a crate.

Drop your audio files in. Name each slot. Download one .bin file, ready to put on chain at ethfs.xyz. Everything happens in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop audio files here or click to choose · mp3, ogg, opus, wav, m4a

Slot names are how a score refers to your sounds. For a pitched instrument use note names like C3, F#4. For a drum kit use whatever the sound is, like kick or snare. Names are taken from your filenames, edit any of them.
